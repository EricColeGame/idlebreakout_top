## Frequently Asked Questions About Idle Breakout Power Ups

### Which ball offers the best return on investment among Idle Breakout power ups?
For early to mid-game progression, the Plasma ball provides the highest return on investment due to its area-of-effect splash damage against dense brick clusters. In the late-game (levels 500+), the Sniper ball becomes the most critical asset, efficiently eliminating high-health anchor bricks that stall automated stage clears.

### When should I prestige to maximize permanent power ups?
You should execute your first prestige reset once stage progress slows noticeably—typically between levels 200 and 300. If clearing a single stage takes several minutes and coin upgrades yield negligible damage increases, converting your progress into Black Bricks provides the permanent multipliers needed to push significantly deeper on your next attempt.

### Are clicking power ups worth investing in during late-game runs?
Clicking upgrades provide excellent early-game momentum to accelerate your first few ball purchases, but their utility drops off sharply after level 200. Late-game brick durability scales into millions of hit points, making manual clicking impractical compared to automated ball DPS and prestige multipliers.

### How do save imports affect unlocked power ups and achievements?
Importing a save string replaces your active game state with the data encoded in the text file, instantly unlocking the power ups, levels, and Black Brick totals associated with that save. While useful for testing endgame builds, importing unverified saves can overwrite your hard-earned manual progress, so always export and back up your current save string before importing new data.
