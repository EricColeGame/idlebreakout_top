Gold bricks feature higher durability than normal bricks but grant substantially larger cash payouts upon destruction. Instead of providing minor per-hit currency, they serve as high-priority bounty targets designed to accelerate your upgrade cycle.

The Sniper ball is generally considered the most effective counter to **idlebreakout gold bricks** because its targeting AI automatically prioritizes high-health objectives. A balanced build is ideal, but damage takes priority whenever durable bricks fail to break quickly. Once your balls can destroy a gold brick in just a few hits, shift your resources into speed and coverage upgrades to reach the next wave of targets faster.
