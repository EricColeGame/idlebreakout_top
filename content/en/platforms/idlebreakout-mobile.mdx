## Managing Save Files and Import Codes on Mobile Devices

One of the greatest strengths of the game is its text-based save import system. This feature allows you to export your progress from a desktop computer and import it into your mobile browser without registering an account. However, copying thousands of characters on a smartphone touchscreen can lead to common formatting errors.

When transferring a save file to your mobile session, ensure your clipboard captures the entire string without leading or trailing spaces. Mobile keyboards often inject automatic spaces after punctuation, which will invalidate the code when pasted into the game's import dialog.

| Issue on Mobile | Primary Cause | Recommended Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Invalid Save" Error** | Truncated string or accidental space | Clear text field, recopy from source, and paste via long-press |
| **Progress Overwritten** | Importing an outdated text snippet | Always export your active mobile save before importing a new one |
| **Lag After Import** | High-level save with excessive active entities | Lower graphics/sound settings in the options menu immediately |
| **Browser Cache Wipe** | Clearing mobile cookies deletes local storage | Keep a text backup of your export string in a notes app |

### Step-by-Step Save Import on Mobile

1. Open the game in your mobile browser or home screen shortcut.
2. Tap the settings gear icon located in the upper menu.
3. Tap the **Import** button to summon the text prompt.
4. Long-press the text box and select **Paste** to insert your complete string.
5. Tap confirm and inspect your cash and ball levels to ensure the transfer succeeded.

Always back up your active session before testing experimental codes or community save files. If you load an imported save designed for level 10,000, you will skip the entire progression curve, eliminating the fun of gradually upgrading your arsenal.

## Troubleshooting Mobile Performance and Battery Heat

Running intensive browser simulations with hundreds of active physics collisions can warm up mobile chipsets during long sessions. If you notice frame drops or device heating, community testing highlights several immediate adjustments:

* **Mute In-Game Audio:** Turning off continuous sound effects frees up mobile WebAudio processing threads.
* **Reduce Screen Brightness:** Lowering display luminance drastically lowers device temperatures during extended idle runs.
* **Close Background Apps:** Free up mobile RAM to prevent your browser from abruptly refreshing the active tab.
* **Toggle Low Power Mode Wisely:** While power-saving modes conserve battery, they can throttle mobile CPU clock speeds, slightly slowing down physics calculations.

By fine-tuning these settings, your phone will remain cool while crushing thousands of bricks without stutter.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Idle Breakout Mobile

### Can I play idlebreakout mobile without an active internet connection?
Yes. Once you load the game in your mobile browser, the assets remain stored in your browser's temporary cache. As long as you do not clear your browsing history or hard-refresh the page while disconnected, you can continue playing offline. However, saving your text export string to your device's notes app is strongly advised in case the tab refreshes.

### How do I stop my phone screen from sleeping while playing?
Most mobile devices automatically dim and lock the display after two to five minutes of inactivity. To keep your game running actively on an idle desk setup, adjust your smartphone settings: set auto-lock to "Never" on iOS (Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock) or extend the screen timeout on Android (Settings > Display > Screen Timeout).

### Does idlebreakout mobile support cross-platform save transfers?
Yes. Because the game relies on text-based save strings, you can seamlessly migrate between desktop and mobile. Simply click the export button on your desktop, email or message the code string to your phone, and paste it into the mobile import prompt under settings.

### What is the best strategy if my mobile run gets stuck before level 1,000?
If progress grinds to a halt, diagnose the board state. If individual bricks take dozens of hits to break, your ball damage is inadequate. If your balls hit hard but wander aimlessly without finding targets, invest heavily in speed and coverage. When playing on touch screens, giving high-health bricks a quick burst of manual taps can jumpstart your automated balls into the next economic tier.
