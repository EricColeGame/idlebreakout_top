## Ball Archetypes and How They Function in Idle Breakout

Every ball in Idle Breakout fills a distinct mechanical niche. While beginners often assume that the most expensive ball unlocked is automatically superior, real game performance depends heavily on brick density, individual brick HP, and movement speed. 

Each ball archetype carries specialized behaviors:
*   **Basic Ball:** The initial workhorse of your run. It deals straightforward damage with standard physics and low scaling costs.
*   **Plasma Ball:** Unleashes explosive area-of-effect (AoE) splash damage upon impact, softening adjacent bricks and clearing clustered formations.
*   **Sniper Ball:** Ignores closer obstacles to specifically seek out and hit the highest-health bricks on screen, delivering heavy single-target burst hits.
*   **Scatter Ball:** Splits into smaller, high-velocity sub-projectiles upon contacting bricks, delivering unprecedented board coverage.
*   **Cannon Ball:** Hits with crushing base damage, ideal for shredding dense armor, though balanced by slower base traversal.
*   **Poison Ball:** Coats targets in corrosive damage-over-time (DoT) debuffs, dealing percentage-based or tick attrition that weakens durable targets across the screen.

## Diagnosing Bottlenecks: When to Upgrade vs. When to Buy New Balls

A common dilemma in Idle Breakout is deciding whether to buy another ball or reinvest in your current roster's statistics. Progress slowdowns generally stem from three distinct issues: insufficient damage, sluggish attack speed, or inadequate board coverage.

Player experience and community reports consistently highlight that identifying the visual symptoms of a board stall prevents wasted resources:

1.  **High-Health Survivors:** If your balls bounce continuously off a brick without breaking it, you are suffering from a damage bottleneck. Upgrading base damage on your primary hitting balls or unlocking a Sniper Ball resolves this issue faster than buying additional low-damage units.
2.  **Long Travel Dead Times:** If bricks break quickly upon impact but balls spend seconds floating across empty spaces, you have a speed and coverage bottleneck. Upgrading ball velocity or adding a Scatter Ball fills empty sectors with hits.
3.  **Uneven Stage Clear Times:** If initial waves vanish in seconds but the final two bricks take thirty seconds to break, your units lack targeting efficiency. A single Sniper Ball eliminates this specific slowdown entirely.

| Balls travel sluggishly between hits | Low attack speed | Speed stat across all active balls | Upgrade existing ball speed stats |

Understanding these symptoms ensures that every coin spent directly targets your run's current bottleneck rather than providing marginal, unfocused improvements.
