## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is the idlebreakout plasma ball considered superior to standard balls for late-game?
The plasma ball delivers potent area-of-effect splash damage upon impact. Standard balls only hit one brick per bounce, which causes severe slowdowns on late-stage boards containing hundreds of high-health bricks. The plasma ball's ability to soften and destroy clustered blocks simultaneously makes it essential for late-game progression.

### What should I upgrade first after unlocking the plasma ball?
Prioritize base damage until the ball can eliminate standard bricks in one or two impacts. Once your damage clears bricks cleanly, shift investment into velocity and speed so the ball completes more bounces and triggers more splash explosions per minute.

### Can I reach level 1000 using only plasma balls?
While a plasma-heavy roster is extremely strong, combining plasma balls with poison balls or sniper balls is significantly more efficient. Poison debuffs weaken high-durability blocks in advance, enabling your plasma explosions to clear entire clusters much faster than an unassisted plasma build could manage alone.

### What causes my run to stall even with an upgraded plasma ball?
Runs typically stall due to an imbalance between damage and speed, or because the board layout features stray, isolated blocks that avoid splash damage. If your balls hit hard but rarely connect, boost movement velocity or add a scatter ball to guarantee complete board coverage.
